How to Contact Me

Please email me at paul.temp@outlook.com.

You should be aware that emails are not necessarily secure, so you should take care with what you write. There is no guarantee that your email will be transferred from you to me along secure links. It could be stored on several servers during transfer. Your email could be stored on several servers en route.

The email address is temporary. If I start getting a lot of spam, it’ll change.

If I need to reply, I'll use my usual email address.
